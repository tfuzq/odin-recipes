Three-Ingredient Baked Chicken Breasts

This baked chicken breast is easy to make with just a few ingredients. I use this basic recipe when another recipe calls for cooked chicken. It works great for everything and is super customizable. Sometimes I use garlic salt or other seasonings, depending on what I'm using it for.

By Jennifer Jones

Image of Three-Ingredient Baked Chicken Breasts


Prep Time: 10 mins Cook Time: 30 mins Total Time: 40 mins

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Lightly butter a baking dish.
  2. Stir together melted butter and salt in a bowl.
  3. Arrange chicken in the prepared baking dish. Brush butter mixture onto chicken until thoroughly coated, pouring any extra over chicken.

ingredients

  • ¼ cup butter, melted
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 4 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
